MagLink · SEM 2026/2027 Consortium Plan

Consortium Structure, Group Contributions & Resource Plan

Group-specific technical contributions (§1.9), cross-group interface alignment, Work Package effort allocation (§5.1), and consortium complementarity (§5.2) across MagLink Core Team (Grupo 2) and Suspensão Ativa Preditiva at DEM, Universidade de Aveiro.

1. Group-Specific Contributions & Reciprocal Alignment (§1.9)

Each participating SEM group owns a distinct engineering layer of the MagLink proposal, matched directly to its published research scope so that deliverables align across both groups’ work plans.

Table 1B — Reciprocal Task Mapping: Matching Suspensão Ativa Preditiva’s Stated Pillars to MagLink
Stated Research Pillar Partner Capability (Their Site & Poster) How It Contributes to MagLink (WP3 & WP5)
1. Computer Vision Topography Real-time 3D road topography reconstruction with a millisecond (ms) preview window before wheel impact. Scans both urban street irregularities (feeder comfort) and guideway entry/exit transition ramps ahead of bogie engagement so leveling actuation starts before rail contact.
2. Vehicle Dynamics & Reverse Roll Complex 4-wheel suspension kinematics, inter-wheel load transfer, and reverse-roll lateral grip stabilization. Manages dynamic chassis load transfer, heave, and pitch across all four independent wheels while steering into curve-constrained motorway lanes and retracting/deploying wheels.
3. High-Frequency Predictive Control (MPC) Speed-dependent Model Predictive Control (MPC) computing pre-impact suspension forces [5–7] to resolve the comfort-vs.-stability trade-off. Executes millimeter-scale chassis attitude control during the road-to-Maglev handoff to hold the vehicle within the target magnetic airgap capture band on entry and wheel touchdown.
4. Energy Harvesting & AI Diagnostics Kinetic vibration energy harvesting to power sensors plus AI diagnostics distinguishing normal road vibration from structural faults. Offsets sensor/actuator power consumption inside WP1/WP4’s vehicle energy budget and supplies early fault detection of suspension anomalies before mode transition locks in WP5 safety trials.

3. Consortium Quality & Complementarity (§5.2)

Grupo 2 · System & Transit

Bimodal Architecture & LCA

Covers structural wheel-to-Maglev chassis packaging, passive mechanical fallback, V2I skip-stop dispatch, and corridor lifecycle carbon accounting [1–4, 16–22].

Partner · Active Control

Predictive Suspension & Airgap

Brings dedicated expertise in 3D road-topography preview, reverse-roll dynamics, speed-dependent MPC, and vibration energy harvesting required to stabilize mode handoff [5–7].

Joint Outcome · TRL 2 → 4

Zero Overlap, Closed Validation Loop

Separating chassis kinematics (WP2) from active leveling control (WP3) avoids duplicated effort and converges directly into shared WP5 sub-scale rig verification.
