2. Work Package Ownership & Resource Allocation (§5.1)

Each Work Package defines clear lead ownership, supporting partner interfaces, and handoff deliverables across the 24-month TRL 2 → TRL 4 timeline.

WP5 · Sub-Scale Rig & TRL 4 Validation M15–M24 Full Consortium (Joint) Joint benchtop/sub-scale transition rig, fault-injection trials & final report Pending sign-off (Issue #6)

WP2 ↔ WP3 Interface & Caderno de Encargos

WP2 → WP3 Inputs

Chassis & Transition Boundary

WP2 supplies sprung/unsprung mass distribution, wheel-retraction stroke envelope, and guideway entry speed band so WP3 can size actuator force and preview horizon.

WP3 → WP2/WP5 Outputs

Airgap Capture & Leveling

WP3 supplies pre-impact 4-wheel MPC pitch/heave stabilization, target airgap tolerance window during engagement, and multi-body dynamic load profiles [5–7].

Pending Spec Lock · Issue #5

Quantitative KPI Targets

Exact numerical targets for transition speed, suspension stroke, transition time window, and mass budget are tracked in Issue #5.

3. Consortium Quality & Complementarity (§5.2)

Grupo 2 · System & Transit

Bimodal Architecture & LCA

Covers structural wheel-to-Maglev chassis packaging, passive mechanical fallback, V2I skip-stop dispatch, and corridor lifecycle carbon accounting [1–4, 16–19].

Partner · Active Control

Predictive Suspension & Airgap

Brings dedicated expertise in 3D road-topography preview, speed-dependent MPC, and independent 4-wheel actuation required to stabilize mode handoff [5–7].

Joint Outcome · TRL 2 → 4

Zero Overlap, Closed Validation Loop

Separating chassis kinematics (WP2) from active leveling control (WP3) avoids duplicated effort and converges directly into shared WP5 sub-scale rig verification.
